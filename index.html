FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about entering and growing in the Saudi market with Alliance.

Ask Us Directly
How can Alliance help me enter the Saudi market?

We begin by conducting a deep-dive analysis of your industry, competitive landscape, and regulatory environment. Based on this, we develop a customized market entry strategy aligned with your objectives, ensuring a smooth and strategic launch into Saudi Arabia.

What are the steps for licensing and establishing a business in Saudi Arabia?

We guide you through every phase of business setup—from selecting the right legal structure and registering your entity to securing all necessary licenses, permits, and tax registrations. Our team ensures compliance and accelerates approvals.

Do I need a local partner to establish my business?

This depends on your industry and business model. We evaluate your case individually. If a local partner is required, we connect you with vetted partners who provide both legal cover and operational alignment.

Can Alliance assist with funding?

Absolutely. We connect you with institutional funding opportunities through entities such as the Public Investment Fund, regional banks, and private equity investors. We also help position your business to meet eligibility and investor expectations.

How can I access government projects or tenders?

We help you register on key government procurement platforms, ensure you meet qualification criteria, support proposal development, and manage engagement with public sector stakeholders to increase your chances of success.

What are the most promising investment sectors in Saudi Arabia right now?

We continuously monitor and identify high-growth sectors including infrastructure, clean energy, artificial intelligence, logistics, healthcare, education, and sports—offering tailored insights and introductions.

Does Alliance offer local representation services?

Yes. We provide permanent, on-the-ground representation for your business—liaising with government entities, managing official meetings, and supporting strategic partnerships to ensure your interests are fully represented.

How long does it take to establish a business in Saudi Arabia?

The full setup process typically takes 4 to 6 weeks, depending on your readiness and the complexity of regulatory approvals. Our in-market experience helps minimize delays and streamline the timeline.

Are your services limited to consulting?

Not at all. We’re a full-service partner—supporting you from initial strategy through market entry, local operations, business development, and performance tracking. Our goal is to ensure your long-term growth in Saudi Arabia.

What makes Alliance different?

Our edge lies in our deep local expertise, broad network of trusted partners, and a flexible engagement model. We’re led by a seasoned Saudi team and supported by global advisors, offering a rare blend of local insight and international standards.
