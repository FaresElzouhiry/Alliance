FAQ

Questions, answered.

How can Alliance help me enter the Saudi market?

We begin with a deep-dive analysis of your industry, competitive landscape and regulatory environment, then build a customised market entry strategy aligned with your objectives.

Do I need a local partner to establish my business?

It depends on your industry and business model. If a local partner is required, we connect you with vetted partners who provide both legal cover and operational alignment.

Can Alliance assist with funding?

Yes. We connect you with institutional funding through entities such as the Public Investment Fund, regional banks and private equity investors.

How long does it take to establish a business?

Typically 4 to 6 weeks, depending on your readiness and the complexity of regulatory approvals.
